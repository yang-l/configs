# Word swaps

Adapted from ste80-humanizer by pnocera (https://github.com/pnocera/ste80, MIT licence).

These tables give plain words for heavy or machine-sounding words. They are a short working list, not the ASD-STE100 dictionary.

- Use a swap when it reads naturally and keeps the meaning.
- Keep a precise technical term even when a table lists it, such as "execute" for code.
- Some words have more than one swap. Pick the swap that fits the context.
